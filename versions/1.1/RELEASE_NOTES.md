# Global BizJet Ops 1.1 Release Notes

Frozen on 2026-07-29.

## Included

- Google Maps default renderer with Advanced Marker aircraft and airport markers.
- Top/bottom map drag bounds and smooth fractional wheel zoom.
- Map contrast mask at 10% to 15% opacity between basemap and markers.
- Static business jet and airport data.
- Five business jet icon classes: `light`, `midsize`, `super-midsize`, `long-range`, `ultra-long`.
- Zoom-responsive aircraft icon sizing from zoom `2.0` to `12.0`.
- Custom equivalent aircraft icon asset pack under `assets/aircraft-icons/custom-equivalent/`.
- Aircraft and airport selected states with left detail panel interactions.
- Business Jet-only filter.
- 1.1 PRD and icon interaction specification under `docs/`.

## Not Included

- Live flight data.
- Database integration.
- In-page third-party member login.
- Third-party brand exposure or unlicensed third-party icon assets.
